Concise Content Extraction (Text Summarization) plays a vital role in modern-day life,providing crucial information about the content. The goal is to present the essential points without unnecessary details, making the content easier to understand and quicker to read. Summarize the lengthy passage into a simple short passage with main sentences. It will provides a simple meaning about the data. Python provides a comprehensive set of tools, resources, and libraries for tasks .NLTK (Natural Language Toolkit) is a popular open-source library used for Natural Language Processing (NLP) tasks in Python.
The goal of this project is to create a robust and efficient text summarization model that can handle diverse content types, such as news articles, scientific papers, and social media posts. The project investigates the use of advanced machine learning algorithms, such as deep learning models and transformer-based architectures (e.g., TF-IDF, BERT, GPT), to improve the quality of the generated summaries in terms of coherence, fluency, and informativeness.
Summarization is useful in real-world examples in a variety of ways. Here are a few examples:
News aggregation Summarization can be used to generate summaries of news articles, allowing
users to quickly get the main points of the news without having to read each article in full.
Research papers, legal agreements, or technical manuals are examples of large or complex texts
that can be summarized. This can make it easier for people to understand the document and
find the information they need. Social media analysis Summarization can be used to analyze
large volumes of social media data, such as tweets or Facebook posts. This can help businesses
to understand customer sentiment, track trends, and identify potential problems. Large
company data sets, such as sales data, customer data, or product data, can be analyzed using
business intelligence summarization. This can help businesses to identify trends, make better
decisions, and improve their operations. Education Summarization can be used to help students
learn and understand complex concepts. For example, a student could use summarization to
generate a summary of a chapter in a textbook or a lecture. This can help the student to identify
the main points of the material and better understand the concepts. There are two sorts of
summary in the context of text: Extractive Selecting and removing specific sentences or phrases
from the source text to create a summary is known as summarization. Extractive summarization
methods often involve natural language processing (NLP) techniques such as sentence ranking
algorithms, keyword extraction, and sentence clustering. On the other hand, abstractive
summarizing entails creating a summary that could include terms and phrases that aren't found
in the original text.
